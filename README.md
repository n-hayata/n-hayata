### What I do

- 🏢 **株式会社トリプルスリー (TRIPLE THREE)** — an HR-tech company for the food-service industry, on a mission of 「外食の人財課題に本気で向き合う」 (founded 2018, ~50 people)
- 🍽️ We ship **9 SaaS products** used by **100+ restaurant companies**, covering the full employee lifecycle — recruiting, onboarding, engagement, and store quality
- ⚡ Our product team is **4 engineers + 1 designer**. Tiny team, AI-native workflow — that's the whole trick
- 🧭 I own product development, tech strategy, engineering-org design, and corporate IT
